## About the project

The Blowout Preventer (BOP) is the most critical safety equipment in oil drilling operations. Failures in this equipment can cause large-scale environmental catastrophes. The project developed two complementary solutions:

### Digital Twin

High-fidelity simulator built in Matlab/Simulink with specialized toolboxes (Curve Fitting, Optimization, Simscape). The model replicates the physical behavior of the BOP under different operating conditions, allowing failure modes to be simulated without real risk.

### BOP Data Universe

Business Intelligence environment integrating sensor data (more than 100 tags) from multiple platforms of partner companies Petrobras, Constellation and Transocean. Includes:

- Preventive alert system based on CBM (Condition-Based Maintenance)
- Text mining in failure records with NLTK and Scikit-Learn
- Statistical survival analysis for component service life
- Alert module integrated with Intelie Live (RTO Live)

## Impact

The project contributed to reducing failure diagnosis time and increasing the predictability of preventive maintenance on monitored equipment.
