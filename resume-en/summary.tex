Senior Data Scientist \& ML Engineer focused on \textbf{transforming data into products that have real impacts, especially in business process agility gains}. I work from planning and designing solutions for projects to developing products in data science and machine learning engineering, as well as mentoring professionals in this area. I am a solution developer with a very business process-oriented perspective, always seeking to understand what the client needs and how it's possible to help.

Some of my experiences include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Ambev Tech:} I delivered intelligent agents for the Legal department using Python, LLMs (GPT-4o/GPT-4o-mini), and OCR with Tesseract, including custom pre-processing for legal documents. The solutions were integrated into the internal Asimov Foundation framework on Azure, orchestrated via Azure Service Bus, and exposed as versioned REST APIs with observability, CI/CD, and data governance using PostgreSQL and Azure Blob Storage.
  \item \textbf{State Government - GovTech | SEGER, SESA, SEDU, SEJUS, SESP, SECONT, Civil Police (ES) \& SEPOL, DETRAN (RJ):} Due to management's trust, I was able to act as Solution Architect for many GovTech projects, architected complete Data Lakehouses using Cloudera Data Platform (HDFS/Impala/Ranger/Kerberos) + PySpark + Airflow. I built automated ETL pipelines in all layers (Bronze, Silver, Gold), implemented AI models for anomaly detection in public prices, automatic document classification, entity extraction via NER and demand forecasting. I guided CI/CD practices, versioning and MLOps for operationalizing ML models in production, and delivered RAG-based AI agents for procurement and legal advisory units within the State Administration Secretariat. Additionally, I participated in the development of more than 50 data products, such as intelligence dashboards and robots for task automation.
  \item \textbf{Eletrobrás Group | Furnas, Chesf, Eletrosul, Eletronorte, Eletrobras Holding:} I designed and developed an integrated legal Data Mart in SAS Viya with analytical dashboards for legal intelligence. I built predictive models for projecting legal process volume, closure detection via NLP and content-based textual classification. I implemented a hybrid SAS Compute + PySpark architecture for distributed processing of large document volumes and automated ML model deployment.
  \item \textbf{Court of Accounts:} I developed crawlers for automated collection of official gazettes (municipalities and state), implemented NLP models with NER for entity extraction in bidding notices and contract extracts, structured data in PostgreSQL and fed SAS Visual Investigator (ElasticSearch + CAS) as an investigative platform for auditors, including alert rules for detecting fraud indicators in bidding processes.

I built the "BOP Data Universe" BI environment integrating multiple data sources, implemented text mining in failure records via NLTK/Scikit-Learn, and created a preventive alert system based on CBM (Condition-Based Maintenance) with clustering and predictive models for IoT/sensor analysis. Finally, I worked on a Digital Twin project for Petrobras' Intelligent Completion System.
\end{itemize}
